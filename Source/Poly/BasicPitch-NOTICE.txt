BasicPitch.cpp / BasicPitchWeights.h contain a C++ port of, and the trained weights from,
Spotify's Basic Pitch (https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch), model "icassp_2022" (nmp.onnx).

Changes: re-implemented in C++ (no ONNX runtime); weights exported unchanged by tools/bptrain/export_cpp.py.

Paper: Bittner et al., "A Lightweight Instrument-Agnostic Model for Polyphonic Note Transcription
and Multipitch Estimation", ICASSP 2022.
